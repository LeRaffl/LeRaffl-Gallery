Privacy Policy — Personal Oura Integration

Effective: 2026-09-06 · Contact: Raphael Wellmann (@leRaffl)

This OAuth application (“App”) is a personal, non-commercial integration used only by the account owner to read their own Oura Ring data for a private personal knowledge base (Vault/Wiki) and related local assistants.

What we collect

Only Oura User Data the owner authorizes via Oura OAuth scopes (for example daily summaries, sleep, activity, heart rate, workouts). No data from other Oura users.

How we use it

  • Summarize trends for the owner’s private Vault (health context).
  • Operate local/read-only tools on devices the owner controls.

What we do not do

  • No sale, rent, or share of Oura data with advertisers or unrelated third parties.
  • No training or fine-tuning of AI models on Oura User Data.
  • No public display of raw Oura metrics.

Storage & retention

OAuth tokens are stored locally on the owner’s machines. Derived notes in the private Vault follow data-minimization: keep only what is needed for personal memory; delete on request. Raw API payloads are not kept longer than needed to produce those notes.

Your rights

The owner can revoke access anytime in the Oura account / app authorizations. Upon revoke or delete request, local tokens and derived notes that are no longer needed are removed.

Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-09-06 · Operator: Raphael Wellmann (@leRaffl)

This policy covers the operator’s public websites (including the BEV Trajectories Gallery) and personal software integrations the operator runs for private use (including OAuth apps that connect to third-party APIs).

Public websites (e.g. Gallery)

  • Pages are informational. We do not require an account to view charts.
  • The operator does not actively or intentionally log visitor data.
  • The only logging that may occur is whatever Cloudflare applies natively when serving the Gallery (for example basic request metadata such as IP as part of Cloudflare’s own infrastructure). The operator does not run additional analytics or intentional IP logging on top of that.
  • We do not sell visitor data and do not intentionally place advertising trackers.

Personal integrations & private tools

Some apps registered under this domain exist only for the operator. They may read the operator’s own data from third-party services the operator connects to maintain a private personal knowledge base.

  • Whose data: only the operator’s, with their consent via the provider’s OAuth/login.
  • Use: private notes, summaries, and automation on devices the operator controls.
  • Not done: selling personal data; sharing with advertisers; training or fine-tuning AI models on connected third-party personal data; publishing raw private metrics.
  • Retention: tokens stored locally; derived notes kept only as needed for personal memory; raw API payloads not retained longer than needed to produce those notes.
  • Revocation: disconnect the app in the provider’s account settings; request deletion via contact below.

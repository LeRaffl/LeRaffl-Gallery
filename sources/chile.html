The fit is provisional: its 80 % date moved by 6 years across recent re-fits. The interval is for the fitted line, point by point, and holds only if the transition keeps following an S-shape. How the intervals are calculated.

The gallery fits a generalized Weibull curve to the BEV share of each period: share(t) = 1 − exp(v1 · (t − t0)v2), t in calendar years. For Chile: v1 = −2.795 × 10−9, v2 = 6.489, t0 = 2014, fitted through September 2026, model run 2026-10-27. This is the project's own model, not an official forecast. A policy change or a stall is not in it.

Where the numbers come from

New-vehicle sales for Chile from ANAC, the national automotive association.

How it works

Chile's figures come from ANAC, which publishes two monthly PDFs — «Mercado Automotor» (the overall market) and «Informe Cero y Bajas Emisiones» (the zero-/low-emission detail). The fetcher takes the total from the first and the BEV/PHEV/HEV split from the second, and only writes a month once both are out.

Combustion is reported as one aggregate ICE figure; petrol and diesel are not split, so those columns stay empty.

Render

Each new or changed month re-renders the country. Shares are read from the CSV as column ÷ TOTAL and grouped into three curves. A generalized Weibull S-curve is fitted to the BEV and the ICE share, and the charts, the fitted parameters, the twelve-month totals and the uncertainty bands are rewritten.

Which curve does a CSV column count towards?
  • BEV→BEV
  • PHEV or EREV (plug-in hybrids, range extenders)
    Does the source split plug-ins from other hybrids?
    • yes→PHEV
    • no — one combined hybrid figure (parked in HEV)→ICE
      the PHEV curve is left out, never drawn at zero
  • HEV or MHEV (full and mild hybrids — no plug)→ICE
  • petrol, diesel, gas, flex-fuel, others→ICE

Caveats

  • Association aggregate: ANAC counts member brands.
  • Combustion is not reported at all: we derive ICE as TOTAL − BEV − PHEV − HEV − OTHERS. There is no petrol/diesel split, so those columns stay empty.
  • The two monthly PDFs are not always published on the same day, so a month only appears once the second one lands.

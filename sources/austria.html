The interval is for the fitted line, point by point, and holds only if the transition keeps following an S-shape. How the intervals are calculated.

The gallery fits a generalized Weibull curve to the BEV share of each period: share(t) = 1 − exp(v1 · (t − t0)v2), t in calendar years. For Austria: v1 = −8.752 × 10−5, v2 = 3.000, t0 = 2011, fitted through October 2026, model run 2026-11-10. This is the project's own model, not an official forecast. A policy change or a stall is not in it.

Render

Each new or changed month re-renders the country. Shares are read from the CSV as column ÷ TOTAL and grouped into three curves. A generalized Weibull S-curve is fitted to the BEV and the ICE share, and the charts, the fitted parameters, the twelve-month totals and the uncertainty bands are rewritten.

Which curve does a CSV column count towards?
  • BEV→BEV
  • PHEV or EREV (plug-in hybrids, range extenders)
    Does the source split plug-ins from other hybrids?
    • yes→PHEV
    • no — one combined hybrid figure (parked in HEV)→ICE
      the PHEV curve is left out, never drawn at zero
  • HEV or MHEV (full and mild hybrids — no plug)→ICE
  • petrol, diesel, gas, flex-fuel, others→ICE

Caveats

  • The source blocks datacenter IPs; data is routed through a Cloudflare Worker relay.
  • PHEV/HEV are split for Whole and Resale; for HDV and Vans hybrids are lumped into HEV.
  • Resale (used registrations) comes from a separate listing page (kfz-gebrauchtzulassungen) whose filenames changed twice across publication eras.
  • {"Resale = Statistik Austria's Pkw-Gebrauchtzulassungen": 'every registration of a used car, mostly changes of owner within Austria (about 3x new registrations), plus used imports. No public table splits the two, so Austria has no used-imports Used variant (09-glossary; renamed from Used in 2026-10,'}

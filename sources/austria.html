Definitions & scope

HEV (full hybrids)split into its own column
BackfillWhole from 2012-01; HDV/Vans from 2024 (annual) + 2025-01 onward; Used from 2019-01

History we hold

Every bar is the span actually present in our stored CSV — breaks in a bar are real gaps in the series, not rendering. Cadence is shown on the right.

Whole 2012-01–2026-08 (176 rows), HDV 2024–2026-08 (21 rows), Vans 2024–2026-08 (21 rows), Used 2019-01–2026-08 (92 rows). Some rows additionally carry a time_interval of annual; the cadence shown is the spacing the periods actually have.

Which fuel columns this source fills

A blank column almost never means "no such cars" — it means this source does not split them out, and those vehicles are counted inside another column. Check the caveats for where they land.

Caveats

  • The source blocks datacenter IPs; data is routed through a Cloudflare Worker relay.
  • PHEV/HEV are split for Whole and Used; for HDV and Vans hybrids are lumped into HEV.
  • Used registrations come from a separate listing page (kfz-gebrauchtzulassungen) whose filenames changed twice across publication eras.

Raw source

Go and check the numbers yourself:

OFV (Opplysningsrådet for veitrafikken) — monthly new-car release, from the Statens vegvesen register ↗

  • OFV statistics — new-car registrations — monthly totals of the year, the fuel mix from January to the last complete month, brand rankings; data from OFV Statistikk / Statens vegvesen
  • OFV news (the monthly release is one of these articles) — one article on the first working day of the next month, with the month's fuel table, top-30 brands and top-30 models as HTML tables since the September 2026 release (earlier releases carried them as images); a second article the next day covers vans
  • OFV's open JSON export (CC BY 4.0) — the same statistics as the page, machine-readable and explicitly allowed for crawlers in OFV's robots.txt — the fetcher's independent cross-check, and its fallback if no release article can be found
  • ACEA — monthly new-car registrations — republishes OFV's figure for Norway about three weeks later; the gallery's Norway source from 2026-04 to 2026-08, and still the fallback writer for a month OFV's fetcher could not fill

Find the release

OFV's monthly article has no fixed address. The fetcher reads OFV's RSS feed (and, as a fallback, its sitemap), opens every article published since the 1st of the next month and keeps the one whose fuel table is for the target month. OFV publishes a van release with the same three tables the next day; it is recognised by its brand table ("varebilmerkene") and skipped.

Powertrain

OFV's table has one row per drivstoff. The fetcher reads the rows by their label, never by position; a label it does not know stops the run until someone decides its column.

  • Bensin plugin hybrid, Diesel plugin hybrid→PHEV

  • Bensin hybrid, Diesel hybrid→HEV
    not chargeable — the figure ACEA reports as HEV for Norway
  • Bensin→PETROL
  • Diesel→DIESEL
  • Hydrogen, gas and other rare fuels→OTHERS

Check it

The rows must add up to the Total, and each printed share must match the counts (a shifted column fails). Two separate OFV publications must then agree exactly — the month's Total with the monthly series of OFV's JSON export, and the article's year-to-date column with the export's fuel mix, fuel by fuel. The year-to-date column minus the months already in the chart must give this month again, and the Total must be within 0.15–4× the same month a year earlier. Only then is the month written — as a new line; the ACEA row for a month is replaced, any other existing row is kept.

Render

Each new or changed month re-renders the country. Shares are read from the CSV as column ÷ TOTAL and grouped into three curves. A generalized Weibull S-curve is fitted to the BEV and the ICE share, and the charts, the fitted parameters, the twelve-month totals and the uncertainty bands are rewritten.

Which curve does a CSV column count towards?
  • BEV→BEV
  • PHEV or EREV (plug-in hybrids, range extenders)
    Does the source split plug-ins from other hybrids?
    • yes→PHEV
    • no — one combined hybrid figure (parked in HEV)→ICE
      the PHEV curve is left out, never drawn at zero
  • HEV or MHEV (full and mild hybrids — no plug)→ICE
  • petrol, diesel, gas, flex-fuel, others→ICE

Definitions & scope

AcquisitionFile — downloaded as a data file (XLSX / Parquet / ODS / zip)
Vehicle scopeFirst registrations of new passenger cars in Norway as OFV counts them from the Statens vegvesen register — all brands, private and company buyers together. Used imports are a separate OFV count and not included.
HEV (full hybrids)OFV publishes seven fuel rows. Elektrisitet is BEV; petrol and diesel plug-in hybrids are PHEV; petrol and diesel hybrids (not chargeable) are HEV; plain petrol and diesel are PETROL and DIESEL. OFV's hybrid rows are what ACEA reports as HEV for Norway — the 2026 year-to-date reconciles to one car — so HEV here means the same as in the other ACEA-anchored countries. Range extenders are registered as plug-in hybrids. Hydrogen and other rare fuels would go to OTHERS, as at ACEA (none in 2026).
BackfillWhole is OFV's figures from 2005-01, transcribed by hand ("ofv.no & ACEA") to 2026-03, ACEA's republished OFV figures for 2026-04..08 (July derived from ACEA's year-to-date, as ACEA had no July release), and read automatically from OFV's release from 2026-09. Vans, HDV and Buses are ACEA's quarterly Commercial Vehicle figures.

Caveats

  • Norway is almost fully electric: 95.9 % of new cars were battery-electric in 2025 and 97.9 % in January–September 2026. The curve is at its ceiling; what still moves is the last few per cent of hybrids and diesels (mostly four-wheel-drive and towing cars).
  • OFV refreshes earlier months a little as late registrations and corrections arrive. The chart keeps every month as first published (OFV's figures today differ from the stored ones by 0–85 cars a month in 2025–2026; 11 of the 17 months January 2025–September 2026 are identical).
  • April and July 2026 come from ACEA: its April figure is 8 cars below OFV's, and July — which ACEA never published on its own — was derived from ACEA's year-to-date, so it is 8 cars too high. The two errors cancel over the year; the fuel split of the two months cannot be corrected, because OFV's month-by-month fuel tables for that period are images.
  • Plug-in hybrids and hybrids are counted as OFV registers them. A December pull-forward ahead of a tax change (2022, 2025) makes single months spiky; read a month against the same month a year earlier.
  • The brand and model tables on this page are every powertrain together — OFV's brand table has no fuel split — and both are OFV's monthly top 30, so the smallest brands are not ranked.

Best source found

Uvoznici vozila — Overview of the New Vehicle Market (M1 / N1 / L) ↗

How it was checked: desk research only — no endpoint exercised yet.

What we found

  • Data model confirmed from three published reports (Q1-2025, H1-2025, H1-2026): first registrations of new M1 cars, sourced from the Ministry of Internal Affairs. N1 vans and L two-wheelers are reported separately.
  • Fuel split: petrol, diesel, hybrids (all types, one bucket), electric, other. H1-2025: 16,473 cars, 161 electric (0.98 %), 6,039 hybrids.
  • Monthly figures exist only as totals in the text; the drivetrain split is quarterly / half-yearly.
  • The national open-data portal data.gov.rs has no Interior Ministry registration dataset (checked 2026-09).

Why it is not on the gallery

  • No PDF, spreadsheet or API — numbers sit in article text, and each period is a new article with an unpredictable URL.
  • PHEV and HEV are not separated.
  • Whether BYD and other Chinese imports appear is not yet confirmed.

What would change the verdict

A stable way to discover the articles (a news index or feed) — then a quarterly parser is feasible. A published table would make it easy.

How it would map, if built

  • Hybrids → HEV (the Türkiye / Georgia combined-hybrid pattern, with the standard footnote); PHEV blank.
  • Possible variants: Vans (N1) and 2-Wheelers (L).

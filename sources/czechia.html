Raw source

Go and check the numbers yourself:

Ministry of Transport — RSV vehicle register, open data (dataovozidlech.cz) ↗

  • Open data — the full register extract (RSV_vypis_vozidel_YYYYMMDD.csv) — one row per vehicle ever registered in Czechia, ~19 million rows, regenerated on the 1st of the month; ~7.5 GB with gzip
  • SDA — Car Importers Association statistics — the figures ACEA publishes for Czechia; monthly tables are members-only except October–December
  • ACEA — monthly new-car registrations — replaces the register's passenger-car row with SDA's official fuel split three weeks later

Download

The Ministry publishes the whole register as one CSV, regenerated on the 1st of the month. The fetcher first reads only the response headers — the file name carries the extract's date — and stops there when every CSV already has the newest complete month. Otherwise it streams the file once, gzip-compressed (~7.5 GB), counting as it goes; nothing is written to disk. A dropped connection restarts the stream (the server cannot resume).

▼
Pick the vehicles

Every record carries the EU vehicle category and two dates — the first registration anywhere and the first registration in Czechia. When they are the same day the vehicle was new; otherwise it is a used import. Every vehicle is counted in the month of its first Czech registration.

  • M1 or M1G
    First registered abroad before?
    • no — first registration is the Czech one→Whole
    • yes→Used
  • N1 or N1G
    New?

    • no — used import→in no variant

Powertrain

The fuel line of the registration certificate lists the energy sources (BA petrol, NM diesel, EL electricity, LPG, CNG, …). Whether a petrol- or diesel-electric car can be charged from the grid is recorded in a free-text hybrid class, which is filled for most plug-ins since mid-2025; before that the CO2 value tells them apart.

Fuel line?
  • EL only→BEV
  • with LPG, CNG, hydrogen or E85→OTHERS
  • petrol or diesel + EL
    Hybrid class?
    • OVC-HEV (chargeable)→PHEV
    • NOVC-HEV (not chargeable)→PETROL/DIESEL
      in Whole's register row these become part of the estimated HEV/PETROL/DIESEL
    • none
      Combined CO2 at most 60 g/km?
      • yes→PHEV
      • no→the class most often recorded for the same type-approval variant — PHEV if chargeable, else PETROL / DIESEL
  • BA — petrol→PETROL
  • NM — diesel→DIESEL
▼
Passenger cars and ACEA

SDA's hybrid split comes from its own type catalogue, which the register cannot reproduce. So the register writes a passenger-car month early with exact BEV, PHEV, other-fuel and total counts and an estimated HEV/petrol/diesel split; three weeks later ACEA's release (SDA's numbers) overwrites the whole row.

Render

Each new or changed month re-renders the country. Shares are read from the CSV as column ÷ TOTAL and grouped into three curves. A generalized Weibull S-curve is fitted to the BEV and the ICE share, and the charts, the fitted parameters, the twelve-month totals and the uncertainty bands are rewritten.

Which curve does a CSV column count towards?
  • BEV→BEV
  • PHEV or EREV (plug-in hybrids, range extenders)
    Does the source split plug-ins from other hybrids?
    • yes→PHEV
    • no — one combined hybrid figure (parked in HEV)→ICE
      the PHEV curve is left out, never drawn at zero
  • HEV or MHEV (full and mild hybrids — no plug)→ICE
  • petrol, diesel, gas, flex-fuel, others→ICE

Definitions & scope

AcquisitionFile — downloaded as a data file (XLSX / Parquet / ODS / zip)
Vehicle scopeVehicles in the road register RSV (not the historic-vehicle register RHSV). Vehicles deregistered since are still counted in the month they were first registered.
HEV (full hybrids)Whole's HEV is SDA's (via ACEA) — full and mild hybrids, classified by SDA from the type approval. The register cannot reproduce it (its hybrid flag is filled for part of the cars only, mild hybrids almost never), so a register-written Whole row carries HEV/PETROL/DIESEL as an estimate until ACEA's figure replaces it. Used, Vans, HDV and Buses have no HEV column — full and mild hybrids are inside PETROL/DIESEL. Plug-ins are recognised by the register's hybrid class (OVC-HEV), a combined CO2 of at most 60 g/km, or the class recorded for the same type-approval variant.
BackfillWhole is SDA/ACEA from 2011 (the register writes from 2026-09 on, replaced by ACEA when it publishes). Used, Vans, HDV and Buses are register counts by month from 2016-01; the earlier quarterly ACEA rows of Vans/HDV/Buses are kept in data/Czechia_legacy.csv.

Caveats

  • Register-written Whole rows (from 2026-09, until ACEA publishes the month — and for July, which ACEA never publishes on its own): BEV, PHEV, other fuels and TOTAL are register counts; HEV, PETROL and DIESEL are estimated in the previous ACEA month's proportions, and the row says so in its notes.
  • Until ACEA replaces a register row, the generated posts give no HEV share for that month (ICE includes HEV) — the estimate is not quoted.
  • Vans count about 2–3 % fewer vehicles than SDA/ACEA, and up to a quarter fewer electric vans since 2025 — SDA also counts vans first registered abroad shortly before import, which the register files as used.
  • Vans, HDV and Buses were quarterly ACEA figures before 2026-10 (BEV and PHEV merged into one "electrically chargeable" number); they are now monthly register counts with a BEV/PHEV split, back to 2016.

Freshness. Latest period in our store: 2026-09. Update cadence: twice daily on the 1st–8th — the extract is regenerated on the 1st; one real run a month streams the whole file (one to two hours), every other run reads only the response headers.

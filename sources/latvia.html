CSDD — first registrations from the vehicle register (Transportlīdzekļu dati, atjaunoti biežāk nekā reizi gadā) ↗

  • CSDD — vehicle data updated more often than once a year — XLSX files replaced every month, each with the full history since 2000. Read are "Pirmoreiz reģistrēto TL skaits" (the headline table of first registrations by vehicle group), "Pirmoreiz reģistrēto vieglo automobiļu skaits" (passenger-car records by make, model, fuel, new/used status and keeper type) and "Pirmoreiz reģistrēto autobusu un kravas automobiļu skaits" (the same for lorries and buses, with the EU category)
  • ACEA — monthly new-car registrations — the cross-check of the Whole series (same register) and the fallback for a month CSDD has not published yet

Network path

CSDD's website answers only European internet addresses (a reachability test from servers in about 40 countries on 2026-10-16 connected from every European one and timed out everywhere else). The fetch runs on GitHub's servers in the US, so it does not call CSDD directly but goes through a small relay of this project on Google Cloud Run in Finland (europe-north1). The relay only forwards requests to www.csdd.lv, needs a secret token, and streams the files back byte for byte — the data are exactly CSDD's.

Where does the request to www.csdd.lv come from?
  • GitHub's server in the US, directly→no answer (connection times out)
  • Cloudflare or Deno Deploy relay (also served from the US)→no answer (tried and dropped, 2026-10-16)
  • the project's relay on Google Cloud Run, Finland→CSDD answers — the files go back to the fetch unchanged
▼
Download

The fetcher reads CSDD's statistics page and finds the three files by their link text (the URLs are opaque indexes). It first downloads the headline table (~50 KB); if that has no figure for the target month yet, it stops. Otherwise it downloads the passenger-car records (~70 MB) and the lorry/bus records (~25 MB). A run whose target month is already a CSDD row in every CSV makes no request at all.

▼
Pick the vehicles

Both record files are aggregated to one row per year, month, make, model, model year, fuel, new/used status and keeper type, with a count. The file decides the vehicle group, the status new or used, the keeper type Private or Industry, and in the lorry/bus file the EU category the variant.

  • passenger-car file
    Status (JAUNS)?
    • Jauns — new
      Keeper type (TURETAJS)?
      • FP — natural person→Whole+Private
      • JP — legal person→Whole+Industry
    • Lietots, or "180 dienas" — first registered abroad before→Used
  • lorry / bus file, status Jauns
    EU category?
    • N1, N1G→Vans
    • N2, N3 (incl. G)→HDV
    • M2, M3 (incl. G)→Buses

The register's fuel (cars) or energy source (lorries, buses) decides the column. Plug-in hybrids have their own value; full and mild hybrids do not.

Fuel / energy source?

  • Benzīns→PETROL

Checks before writing

All passenger cars first registered in the month (every status) must equal the headline table's "Vieglie" — a difference above 0.5 % (and 3 cars) in any of the last 13 months stops the run. From 2022 the two match within 4 cars in every month but December 2023. The lorry and bus totals are compared and reported, not enforced (the headline "Kravas" counts more lorry types). Unknown fuel, status or keeper values may not exceed 2 % of a month, and a month below a quarter of the usual volume is not written.

Render

Each new or changed month re-renders the country. Shares are read from the CSV as column ÷ TOTAL and grouped into three curves. A generalized Weibull S-curve is fitted to the BEV and the ICE share, and the charts, the fitted parameters, the twelve-month totals and the uncertainty bands are rewritten.

Caveats

  • Whole equals ACEA's Latvian figure: 2015-01 … 2024-12 the same TOTAL, BEV, PHEV, petrol and diesel in every month; 2025–2026 at most a few cars apart (CSDD books late registrations after ACEA has published). ACEA's OTHERS was sometimes a few cars lower with the same TOTAL.
  • HEV is not reported: full and mild hybrids are inside PETROL/DIESEL. PHEV is plug-in hybrids only, so the PHEV curve is real but the HEV share cannot be shown.
  • Used imports are a market of their own: about 42,000 a year (twice the new market), mostly diesel; 10 % BEV and 5 % PHEV in January–September 2026.
  • Vans run up to about 13 % and HDV up to about 17 % below ACEA's quarterly reconstructions in 2024–2026 (their electric counts agree); ACEA's CV quarters for Latvia also have known errors (HDV 2022-Q2, 2023-Q3). Buses are 0–40 a month, so single deliveries of electric city buses swing the shares.
  • CSDD's website answers only European internet addresses. GitHub's servers (which run the fetch) are in the US, so every request goes through a small relay of this project on Google Cloud Run in Finland, which fetches the files from CSDD and passes them back unchanged. If the relay is down, the fetch fails loudly; ACEA still fills the Whole series.
  • The file is the register as it is today, not a snapshot of each month: more than a year back the record counts drift up to ~1.5 % below the headline table written at the time (records removed since), while new cars still equal ACEA's figure. December 2023 is the exception the other way: the records hold 653 more first-registered cars than the headline table (new cars still equal ACEA).

Freshness. Latest period in our store: 2026-09. Update cadence: twice daily (06:20 and 12:20 UTC) on the 8th–25th; CSDD replaces the files about ten working days after month end.

The registry is a list of every vehicle on the road today, not a list of registrations. A month is counted as the vehicles whose road-entry date falls in it; every run re-counts the last three months, because the newest are still filling up.

The dataset holds nothing else — no motorcycles, no lorries over 3.5 t, no buses.

Powertrain

The registry's fuel value hides most hybrids — a regular hybrid is recorded as plain petrol, and the two electric-plus-fuel values are kept for plug-ins. So every petrol, diesel and electric-plus-fuel car is looked up in the Ministry's model catalogue (WLTP) by maker, model code, model year and trim. Where the trim is missing from the catalogue, the drive technology that most of that model's trims have decides.

Render

Each new or changed month re-renders the country. Shares are read from the CSV as column ÷ TOTAL and grouped into three curves. A generalized Weibull S-curve is fitted to the BEV and the ICE share, and the charts, the fitted parameters, the twelve-month totals and the uncertainty bands are rewritten.

Which curve does a CSV column count towards?
  • BEV→BEV
  • PHEV or EREV (plug-in hybrids, range extenders)
    Does the source split plug-ins from other hybrids?
    • yes→PHEV
    • no — one combined hybrid figure (parked in HEV)→ICE
      the PHEV curve is left out, never drawn at zero
  • HEV or MHEV (full and mild hybrids — no plug)→ICE
  • petrol, diesel, gas, flex-fuel, others→ICE

Caveats

  • The registry is a stock snapshot of currently-registered vehicles, so older months lose deregistered vehicles and undercount slightly; recent months are effectively exact.
  • Regular hybrids are hidden inside the petrol fuel value — HEV/PHEV come from the model-catalogue join (100% coverage back to 2017; the fetcher warns above 5% unmatched).
  • Counts slice by road-entry date, whereas the importers' association reports deliveries, so single months can differ a few percent in either direction.

Freshness. Latest period in our store: 2026-09. Update cadence: daily 08:00 UTC, 10th–20th of the month; self-throttles once the previous month is in.

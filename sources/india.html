How it works

India's figures are pulled — partially and by hand — from the VAHAN dashboard (Ministry of Road Transport & Highways), the national vehicle-registration system. Coverage is incomplete and updates are irregular, so treat India as provisional.

India is the one gallery country with no CSV in this repo: all four variants are still rendered from the maintainer's local pipeline, so there is no raw file to link and no build-time freshness check behind this page. Every other country's numbers can be traced to a committed CSV; India's cannot.

Caveats

  • No committed data file — the series lives outside this repository, so nothing on this page is derived from data you can inspect here.
  • Coverage is partial and updated by hand; the variants can sit months apart and lag well behind other countries.
  • VAHAN registration timing and state coverage differ from the other national sources.

The band is for the fitted line, point by point, and holds only if the transition keeps following an S-shape. How the bands are calculated.

The gallery fits a generalized Weibull curve to the BEV share of each period: share(t) = 1 − exp(v1 · (t − t0)v2), t in calendar years. For Spain: v1 = −3.230 × 10−5, v2 = 3.270, t0 = 2014, fitted through October 2026, model run 2026-11-15. This is the project's own model, not an official forecast. Download

DGT publishes one zip per month with one fixed-width record per registered vehicle (714 characters, 69 fields). One pass over the file fills every variant; a month with more than 1% of records of another length, or a Whole total under 2,000, stops the run.

Until that monthly zip is out (around the 15th), the month is summed from DGT's daily zips instead — same records, one file per registration day — but only once every weekday file (national holidays excepted) is there. Those rows are marked provisional and the monthly zip replaces them.

Pick the vehicles

Cars and two-wheelers are picked by DGT's national vehicle type; vans, lorries and buses by the EU homologation category, which DGT records for them reliably.

Whole is every new vehicle DGT registers as a passenger car — EU class M1 — with no model list on top. It therefore also holds the off-roaders (type 25) and the passenger versions of vans (Multivan, Tourneo, Berlingo, Proace Verso, …, about 2% of the market), which ANFAC — and with it ACEA — counts as light commercials; some DGT-based sites also leave out the off-roaders.

Powertrain

DGT labels every electrified vehicle with an electric-vehicle category. Only where that label is empty does the propulsion code of the technical inspection decide.

Electric-vehicle category?
  • BEV→BEV
  • PHEV→PHEV
  • REEV — range extender→EREV
    folds into the PHEV curve in the charts
  • HEV→HEV
    full and mild hybrids
  • FCEV (fuel cell) or HICEV (hydrogen combustion)→OTHERS
  • none
    Propulsion code?
    • 0 — petrol→PETROL
    • 1 — diesel→DIESEL
    • 2 — electric→BEV
      an electric vehicle without a category label — rare
    • anything else — LPG, CNG, LNG, hydrogen, …→OTHERS

Render

Each new or changed month re-renders the country. Shares are read from the CSV as column ÷ TOTAL and grouped into three curves. A generalized Weibull S-curve is fitted to the BEV and the ICE share, and the charts, the fitted parameters, the twelve-month totals and the uncertainty bands are rewritten.

Which curve does a CSV column count towards?
  • BEV→BEV
  • PHEV or EREV (plug-in hybrids, range extenders)
    Does the source split plug-ins from other hybrids?
    • yes→PHEV
    • no — one combined hybrid figure (parked in HEV)→ICE
      the PHEV curve is left out, never drawn at zero
  • HEV or MHEV (full and mild hybrids — no plug)→ICE
  • petrol, diesel, gas, flex-fuel, others→ICE

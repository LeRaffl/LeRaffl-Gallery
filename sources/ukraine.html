Download

The register comes as one zip per year with one CSV inside, one record per registration operation. The current year is re-uploaded about monthly, cut at the previous month's end; a month is written only once the file covers it to within three days of its end.

▼
Pick the vehicles

Every record carries the MIA's operation code, which says what happened — a first registration of a new vehicle, of a used import, a change of owner, a deregistration. Only first registrations count. Codes 105 and 100 split new from used dealer sales only from 2018-08, so the series starts 2018-09.

Operation code?
  • first registration of a new vehicle — 105 / 99 (from a dealer, imported / made in Ukraine), 72 (imported by the owner), 180 / 184 / 185 (by a business), 74 / 75 / 102 (humanitarian or experimental)
    Vehicle kind?
    • ЛЕГКОВИЙ — passenger car
      Owner type?
      • P — natural person→Whole+Private
      • J — legal person→Whole+Industry
      • blank (or any other value)→Whole
        counted and listed in the run summary
    • ВАНТАЖНИЙ — goods vehicle
      Gross weight at most 3,500 kg?
      • yes→Vans
      • no (or no weight recorded)→in no variant
    • anything else — bus, motorcycle, trailer, …→in no variant
  • first Ukrainian registration of a used vehicle — 100 (from a dealer, imported), 70 / 71 (imported by the owner), 76 / 77 (humanitarian), 172 (the 2018 import wave)
    Vehicle kind ЛЕГКОВИЙ (passenger car)?
    • yes→Used
    • no→in no variant
  • anything else — change of owner, re-registration, deregistration, …→in no variant
    an unknown code whose name reads like a first registration is listed in the run summary (not counted), so a new code the MIA introduces is noticed

Powertrain

The register states the fuel itself; nothing is classified from model names. It has one value for every kind of petrol- or diesel-electric hybrid, so plug-ins cannot be told apart from full or mild hybrids.

Register fuel?
  • ЕЛЕКТРО — electric→BEV
  • electric or petrol, electric or diesel, petrol / gas or electric, gas and electric→HEV
    the register's single hybrid value (plug-in + full + mild), shown as "Hybrid" and counted as ICE — there is no PHEV curve
  • БЕНЗИН — petrol→PETROL
  • ДИЗЕЛЬНЕ ПАЛИВО — diesel→DIESEL
  • petrol or gas, gas, diesel or gas, hydrogen, "not determined", "absent", blank→OTHERS
  • any other value→OTHERS
    above 2 % of a month's Whole the run stops

Render

Each new or changed month re-renders the country. Shares are read from the CSV as column ÷ TOTAL and grouped into three curves. A generalized Weibull S-curve is fitted to the BEV and the ICE share, and the charts, the fitted parameters, the twelve-month totals and the uncertainty bands are rewritten.

Which curve does a CSV column count towards?
  • BEV→BEV
  • PHEV or EREV (plug-in hybrids, range extenders)
    Does the source split plug-ins from other hybrids?
    • yes→PHEV
    • no — one combined hybrid figure (parked in HEV)→ICE
      the PHEV curve is left out, never drawn at zero
  • HEV or MHEV (full and mild hybrids — no plug)→ICE
  • petrol, diesel, gas, flex-fuel, others→ICE

Caveats

  • The register has one combined hybrid fuel value — plug-in, full and mild hybrids cannot be told apart, so there is no PHEV curve and plug-ins sit inside ICE.
  • New vs used comes from the register's operation codes. Code 105 ("new vehicle from a dealer, imported") only exists from August 2018; before that new and used dealer sales shared one code, so the series starts 2018-09.
  • Registrations, not sales. The carmakers' association Ukrautoprom (dealer sales) reports ~3–5 % more new cars; BEV counts agree within ~1 % (see the developer doc).
  • EV imports were VAT-exempt until 31 Dec 2025. December 2025 shows a pull-forward spike and 2026 a sharp drop in both new and used BEV registrations — a policy step, not noise.
  • Used is a different population from new registrations (used imports, dominated by customs policy); read it next to Whole, not as part of it.

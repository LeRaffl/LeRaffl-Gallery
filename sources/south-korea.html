The fit is provisional: its 80% date moved by 19 years across recent re-fits. The band is for the fitted line, point by point, and holds only if the transition keeps following an S-shape. How the bands are calculated.

The gallery fits a generalized Weibull curve to the BEV share of each period: share(t) = 1 − exp(v1 · (t − t0)v2), t in calendar years. For South Korea: v1 = −5.491 × 10−4, v2 = 2.568, t0 = 2016, fitted through October 2026, model run 2026-11-17. This is the project's own model, not an official forecast. A policy change or a stall is not in it.

Where the numbers come from

Monthly domestic vehicle sales by powertrain for South Korea from the Ministry of Trade, Industry and Resources' «자동차산업 동향» (automotive industry trends) press release — domestic makers plus imports, battery-electric, plug-in, hybrid and hydrogen cars broken out.

Render

Each new or changed month re-renders the country. Shares are read from the CSV as column ÷ TOTAL and grouped into three curves. A generalized Weibull S-curve is fitted to the BEV and the ICE share, and the charts, the fitted parameters, the twelve-month totals and the uncertainty bands are rewritten.

Caveats

  • All vehicle types, not only passenger cars (EU M1) — trucks, vans and buses are in the total (the release does not state their share). Compare the level with M1-only countries with care.
  • Sales as reported by the makers' and importers' associations, not registry counts.
  • Imported hybrids include mild hybrids (KAIDA does not separate them before 2025 and MOTIR adds them together); domestic makers' mild hybrids are not in the hybrid figure.
  • Tesla is missing before 2024: KAIDA only counts Tesla from 2024 data on, so BEV and the total are too low up to 2023-12 (roughly 10% of BEV in 2021–2023) — a step at 2024-01. BYD (from 2025) is included. See issue #257.
  • MOTIR revises the previous month in the following release; the series always holds the latest published figure.
  • 2017–2024 were entered by hand from the same releases and are not re-verified automatically; from 2025 every month is parsed and cross-checked (two transcription errors in the hand-entered rows were corrected: 2025-09 and 2026-04).
  • Printed under the charts: Domestic sales of all vehicle types incl. commercial - not M1 registrations. Tesla missing before 2024, so BEV is understated up to 2023. Imported hybrids include mild hybrids.

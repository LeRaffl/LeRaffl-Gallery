Which fuel columns this source fills

A blank column almost never means "no such cars" — it means this source does not split them out, and those vehicles are counted inside another column. Check the caveats for where they land.

Caveats

  • The source reports combustion as one aggregate figure, so the ICE column is what it publishes — there is no petrol/diesel split and those columns stay empty.
  • Access needs a member cookie session (login).
  • A pre-rebase Whole series is archived as an inert legacy CSV.

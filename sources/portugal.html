Render

Each new or changed month re-renders the country. Shares are read from the CSV as column ÷ TOTAL and grouped into three curves. A generalized Weibull S-curve is fitted to the BEV and the ICE share, and the charts, the fitted parameters, the twelve-month totals and the uncertainty bands are rewritten.

Which curve does a CSV column count towards?
  • BEV→BEV
  • PHEV or EREV (plug-in hybrids, range extenders)
    Does the source split plug-ins from other hybrids?
    • yes→PHEV
    • no — one combined hybrid figure (parked in HEV)→ICE
      the PHEV curve is left out, never drawn at zero
  • HEV or MHEV (full and mild hybrids — no plug)→ICE
  • petrol, diesel, gas, flex-fuel, others→ICE

Which fuel columns this source fills

A blank column almost never means "no such cars" — it means this source does not split them out, and those vehicles are counted inside another column. Check the caveats for where they land.

Caveats

  • Full hybrids (HEV) are reported natively (petrol and diesel hybrids).
  • Flexfuel is not reported — the column stays empty.
  • OTHERS is a residual (all-fuels total minus the named fuels).
  • The commercial variants start in 2025/2026 and are still thin: motordata exposes only the current calendar year and there is no commercial history to backfill. They are fetched and committed, but only Whole is rendered on a schedule — a Weibull fit on a handful of points is meaningless.

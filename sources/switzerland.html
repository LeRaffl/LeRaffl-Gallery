Download

ASTRA restates the whole running year in one file on every refresh, one row per vehicle (about 270,000 rows by September). The fetcher first reads only the first 16 KB — the header and one row — to see which month the snapshot is complete to; only when a CSV lacks that month does it download the file (about 90 MB of new registrations, about 15 MB of used imports).

Pick the vehicles

Every record carries the EU vehicle class and the register's vehicle kind (Fahrzeugart); together they decide the variant. Switzerland and Liechtenstein are one register and are counted together, as ACEA does. Vehicles taken off the road since still count in the month they were registered new.

Which register file is the record in?
  • New registrations (NEUZU)
    EU vehicle class?
    • M1 (or blank on a few old records)
      Vehicle kind Personenwagen?
      • yes→Whole
      • no — camper, special vehicle ("light/heavy motor vehicle")→in no variant
    • N1
      Vehicle kind Lieferwagen (van)?
      • yes→Vans
      • no — work machine, small tractor unit→in no variant
    • N2 or N3
      Lorry or tractor unit (Lastwagen, Sattelschlepper)?
      • yes→HDV
      • no — work machine on a truck chassis→in no variant
    • M2 or M3
      Gross weight over 3.5 t?
      • yes→Buses
      • no — minibus→in no variant
    • L1 (moped) or L3 (motorcycle)→2-Wheelers
    • anything else — trikes, quads, trailers, tractors, …→in no variant
  • Used imports (GEBR — first registered abroad)
    Vehicle kind Personenwagen?
    • yes→Used
      dated by the month of the first Swiss registration, not the first one abroad
    • no→in no variant

Powertrain

The register states the fuel and, for hybrids, whether the battery can be charged from the grid. Only vehicles approved under the older national type approval carry no hybrid code (roughly a third of the hybrids in 2026); for them the electric consumption or the CO2 value decides.

Register fuel code?
  • E — electric→BEV
  • R — electric with range extender→PHEV
    range extenders count as plug-ins, as at ACEA (no separate EREV column)
  • C or F — petrol or diesel + electric
    Hybrid code?
    • OVC-HEV — chargeable from the grid→PHEV
    • NOVC-HEV — not chargeable (full and mild hybrids)→HEV
    • none — older national type approval
      Electric consumption recorded?
      • yes→PHEV
      • no
        CO2 at most 60 g/km?
        • yes→PHEV
        • no (or not recorded)→HEV
  • B — petrol→PETROL
  • D — diesel→DIESEL
  • anything else — hydrogen, petrol/gas bi-fuel, …→OTHERS

Month

ACEA's Swiss month is the year-to-date total at a cut-off shortly after month end, minus the previous month's. The fetcher writes a month the same way, so the two agree.

Is the month already in the CSV?
  • yes→left as it is — a written month is never revised
  • no
    Which run writes it?
    • the monthly run, from the first snapshot after the month ends→the month's registrations + late registrations of the year's earlier months
      late = keyed into the register after their month was written (e.g. after a quarter-end push); only months that came from ASTRA count, and January starts fresh
    • the backfill of a closed year (ASTRA's year file)→the month's registrations, by registration date
▼
Check against ACEA

ACEA is the reference. When ACEA publishes the month (third to fourth week), the newest months are compared with ACEA's Swiss figure; a gap above 1 % in the total, or 3 % in one fuel with at least 200 cars, raises a warning. Over 2025-01 to 2026-08 (19 releases, 366,000 cars) the register reproduces ACEA to −0.02 % in total and +0.1 % in BEV.

Render

Each new or changed month re-renders the country. Shares are read from the CSV as column ÷ TOTAL and grouped into three curves. A generalized Weibull S-curve is fitted to the BEV and the ICE share, and the charts, the fitted parameters, the twelve-month totals and the uncertainty bands are rewritten.

Which curve does a CSV column count towards?
  • BEV→BEV
  • PHEV or EREV (plug-in hybrids, range extenders)
    Does the source split plug-ins from other hybrids?
    • yes→PHEV
    • no — one combined hybrid figure (parked in HEV)→ICE
      the PHEV curve is left out, never drawn at zero
  • HEV or MHEV (full and mild hybrids — no plug)→ICE
  • petrol, diesel, gas, flex-fuel, others→ICE

Caveats

  • A month is attributed the way ACEA attributes it: a car keyed into the register after the month closed (late registrations, typically after a quarter-end push) counts in the next month written. Every row is written once and never revised, so monthly figures can differ from a later look at the register by a few hundred cars while the year totals agree.
  • The Whole history before 2022 is BFS (the Federal Statistical Office). BFS also counts used imports, in the month of their first registration abroad, so its months kept growing after publication — by up to 6 % in 2025 as near-new imports from the EU rose. From 2022 the history is rebuilt from ASTRA's register and matches auto-schweiz/ACEA (2022: 40,168 BEV against 40,173); 2026-04..08 are ACEA's figures. The step at 2022 is small (volume −1.6 %, BEV share +0.1 points).
  • Vans, HDV and Buses were quarterly ACEA figures before (with BEV and PHEV merged into one "electrically chargeable" number); they are now monthly register counts with a real BEV/PHEV split, back to 2016. The old ACEA series is kept as an archive (data/Switzerland_legacy.csv).
  • Before 2022 the register has no hybrid code; plug-ins of that period are recognised by CO2 alone, which under-counts some 2019 PHEVs by up to a third (a small series then).

The fit is provisional: its 80% date moved by 13 years across recent re-fits. The interval is for the fitted line, point by point, and holds only if the transition keeps following an S-shape. How the intervals are calculated.

The gallery fits a generalized Weibull curve to the BEV share of each period: share(t) = 1 − exp(v1 · (t − t0)v2), t in calendar years. For USA: v1 = −3.080 × 10−5, v2 = 2.795, t0 = 2009, fitted through October 2026, model run 2026-11-18. This is the project's own model, not an official forecast. A policy change or a stall is not in it.

Where the numbers come from

Monthly new light-vehicle SALES in the United States — cars and light trucks including pickups, SUVs and vans (GVWR up to 10,000 lb) — with battery-electric, plug-in hybrid and hybrid counts, compiled by Argonne National Laboratory (ANL) from Wards Auto. These are sales, not registrations, and pickups cannot be separated out.

Raw source

  • ANL historical data page (link to the PDF the fetcher reads) — one PDF, full monthly history Dec-2010 → latest, columns Month · BEV · PHEV · HEV · Total LDV

How the data flows

Origin
Wards Auto (Wards Intelligence) monthly US light-vehicle sales, compiled by Argonne's Systems Assessment Center for DOE's Vehicle Technologies Office; before Oct-2019 EDTA, Hybrid Dashboard, InsideEVs and Green Car Congress

Fetcher

▼
Find the PDF

The fetcher opens ANL's historical-data page with a browser User-Agent and takes the link whose URL or text contains "total" and "sales" and ends in .pdf ("Total Sales for Website_<Month> <Year>.pdf").

▼
Read the table

pypdf turns the PDF into text; every line that starts with a month tag (e.g. "Aug-25") followed by four numbers is a row — BEV, PHEV, HEV, Total LDV. Legend text after the numbers and the "PEV Sales by Size" box are ignored.

ANL column?
  • BEV→BEV
  • PHEV→PHEV
  • HEV→HEV
  • Total LDV→TOTAL
  • (none — fuel cell)→inside ICE
    OTHERS = 0; FCEVs are a few dozen a month
▼
Which vehicles are counted

ANL's light-duty total is every new car and light truck up to 10,000 lb GVWR sold in the month. Nothing is reclassified.

Vehicle type?
  • Passenger cars (sedans, hatchbacks, coupés, wagons)→Whole
  • SUVs / crossovers, minivans→Whole
  • Pickups and vans ≤ 10,000 lb GVWR→Whole
    not separable in the ANL table
  • Medium/heavy trucks and buses (> 10,000 lb)→in no variant
▼
Check it

Before anything is written the whole table must pass — no missing month between the first and the last, no negative value, BEV + PHEV + HEV not above the total, and the total between 0.5 M and 2.5 M. Any failure stops the run and leaves the CSV untouched. A value identical in two consecutive months, a PDF more than two months behind, and older CSV rows that no longer match the PDF are reported as warnings.

All checks pass?
  • yes→last 3 months written (all months with months=0)
  • False→run fails, CSV untouched
▼
Build the row

ICE = TOTAL − BEV − PHEV − HEV; OTHERS = 0. The file is rewritten only when a value changed, then render-country.yml is dispatched for USA. The exact PDF and its text are kept as a 7-day workflow artifact (anl-total-sales).

Store
data/USA.csv

Render

Each new or changed month re-renders the country. Shares are read from the CSV as column ÷ TOTAL and grouped into three curves. A generalized Weibull S-curve is fitted to the BEV and the ICE share, and the charts, the fitted parameters, the twelve-month totals and the uncertainty bands are rewritten.

Which curve does a CSV column count towards?
  • BEV→BEV
  • PHEV or EREV (plug-in hybrids, range extenders)
    Does the source split plug-ins from other hybrids?
    • yes→PHEV
    • no — one combined hybrid figure (parked in HEV)→ICE
      the PHEV curve is left out, never drawn at zero
  • HEV or MHEV (full and mild hybrids — no plug)→ICE
  • petrol, diesel, gas, flex-fuel, others→ICE

Caveats

  • SALES, not registrations — the US has no free national new-registration feed (state DMVs publish separately; Experian/S&P registration data is paid).
  • Cars + light trucks INCLUDING pickups, SUVs and vans (GVWR ≤ 10,000 lb; EPA's light-duty definition is 8,500 lb). Not comparable 1:1 with EU-M1 passenger-car series. ANL publishes no car/truck split of the table, so a cars-only or pickups series is not possible from this source.
  • Combustion is not reported; ICE = Total − BEV − PHEV − HEV (no petrol/diesel split).
  • Fuel-cell cars are not in the PDF table and stay inside the ICE residual — a few dozen a month (ANL web page, Jul-2025 — 32 Toyota Mirai and 5 Honda CR-V), so OTHERS is 0.
  • ANL revises recent months and sometimes whole earlier years between releases; the fetcher re-writes the last 3 months and warns about any older drift (full refresh with months=0). The full history was refreshed from the Aug-2026 release on 2026-10-18.
  • Source lineage changes in Oct-2019 (Wards Auto as reference); earlier months come from EDTA / Hybrid Dashboard / InsideEVs / Green Car Congress.
  • Printed under the charts: New light-vehicle SALES (not registrations): cars + light trucks incl. pickups, SUVs and vans up to 10,000 lb GVWR. Argonne National Laboratory (ANL), compiled from Wards Auto.

Freshness. Latest period in our store: 2026-10. Update cadence: daily from the 10th to the end of the month, 10:30 UTC — ANL publishes about 6–7 weeks after the month (the Aug-2026 PDF is dated 17 Sep 2026).

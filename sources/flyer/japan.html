How to read it. Battery electric (BEV): battery only. Plug-in hybrid: engine plus a battery charged from the grid. Hybrid: engine plus battery, no plug. Shares are of new registrations.

Source: JADA. Registered passenger cars only; excludes kei cars (about a third of the market) and most kei-BEVs.

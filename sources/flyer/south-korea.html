How it compares

26th of 61 countries by BEV share of new cars, last 12 months. Each line is one country; the middle one is at 14.9%.

How to read it. Battery electric (BEV): battery only. Plug-in hybrid: engine plus a battery charged from the grid. Hybrid: engine plus battery, no plug. Shares are of new registrations.

Source: MOTIR (산업통상부). Domestic sales of all vehicle types incl. commercial - not M1 registrations. Tesla missing before 2024, so BEV is understated up to 2023.…

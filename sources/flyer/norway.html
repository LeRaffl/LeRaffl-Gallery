How it compares

1st of 60 countries by BEV share of new cars, last 12 months. Each line is one country; the middle one is at 14.2%.

How to read it. Battery electric (BEV): battery only. Plug-in hybrid: engine plus a battery charged from the grid. Hybrid: engine plus battery, no plug. Shares are of new registrations.

Source: OFV (Opplysningsrådet for veitrafikken). New passenger cars (OFV). PHEV includes range extenders. Tax changes pull registrations into December and out of January.

How it compares

13th of 61 countries by BEV share of new cars, last 12 months. Each line is one country; the middle one is at 14.9%.

How to read it. Battery electric (BEV): battery only. Plug-in hybrid: engine plus a battery charged from the grid. Hybrid: engine plus battery, no plug. Shares are of new registrations.

Source: CPCA monthly market analysis (月度分析). Passenger cars only. Whole = retail (零售); Wholesale = manufacturer shipments incl. exports.

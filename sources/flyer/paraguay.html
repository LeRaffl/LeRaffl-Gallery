How it compares

56th of 60 countries by BEV share of new cars, last 12 months. Each line is one country; the middle one is at 14.2%.

How to read it. Battery electric (BEV): battery only. Plug-in hybrid: engine plus a battery charged from the grid. Hybrid: engine plus battery, no plug. Shares are of new registrations.

Source: DNIT (customs, ex Dirección Nacional de Aduanas). Customs imports of new cars, not registrations - single months can run ahead of sales. Pickups not included. HEV includes mild hybrids.

How it compares

12th of 60 countries by BEV share of new cars, last 12 months. Each line is one country; the middle one is at 14.2%.

How to read it. Battery electric (BEV): battery only. Plug-in hybrid: engine plus a battery charged from the grid. Hybrid: engine plus battery, no plug. Shares are of new registrations.

Source: FPS Mobility and Transport (FOD Mobiliteit en Vervoer / SPF Mobilité et Transports). HEV includes mild hybrids (about 2.5x ACEA's full-hybrid figure), so petrol/diesel run lower than ACEA's. The April 2025 file is about 13 %…

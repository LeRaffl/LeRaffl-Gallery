How it compares

22nd of 60 countries by BEV share of new cars, last 12 months. Each line is one country; the middle one is at 14.2%.

How to read it. Battery electric (BEV): battery only. Plug-in hybrid: engine plus a battery charged from the grid. Hybrid: engine plus battery, no plug. Shares are of new registrations.

Source: Statistik Austria. Whole = Pkw (M1); HDV = Lkw N2+N3+articulated; Vans = Lkw N1; Resale = Pkw used registrations (owner changes + used imports).

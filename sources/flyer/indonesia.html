How it compares

23rd of 60 countries by BEV share of new cars, last 12 months. Each line is one country; the middle one is at 14.2%.

How to read it. Battery electric (BEV): battery only. Plug-in hybrid: engine plus a battery charged from the grid. Hybrid: engine plus battery, no plug. Shares are of new registrations.

Source: GAIKINDO. Whole = GAIKINDO Passenger Car (≈ EU M1); figures are wholesales, not registrations.

45th of 60 countries by BEV share of new cars, last 12 months. Each line is one country; the middle one is at 14.2%.

How to read it. Battery electric (BEV): battery only. Plug-in hybrid: engine plus a battery charged from the grid. Hybrid: engine plus battery, no plug. Shares are of new registrations.

Source: Transpordiamet. New passenger cars (M1); Transpordiamet register from 2021, ACEA (quarterly) before. HEV includes mild hybrids.

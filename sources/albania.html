Which fuel columns this source fills

A blank column almost never means "no such cars" — it means this source does not split them out, and those vehicles are counted inside another column. Check the caveats for where they land.

Caveats

  • Counts are first registrations, including imported used vehicles — not only new.
  • Data comes from a Looker Studio report that needs a headless-browser session.
  • 2025 non-Whole snapshots are corrupt upstream and were not fetched.

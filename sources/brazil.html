The fit is provisional: its 80 % date moved by 6 years across recent re-fits. No confidence interval is shown: none can be stated reliably for this series (poor fit). How the intervals are calculated.

The gallery fits a generalized Weibull curve to the BEV share of each period: share(t) = 1 − exp(v1 · (t − t0)v2), t in calendar years. For Brazil: v1 = −4.389 × 10−16, v2 = 12.007, t0 = 2011, fitted through September 2026, model run 2026-10-08. This is the project's own model, not an official forecast. A policy change or a stall is not in it.

Where the numbers come from

New registrations of cars and light commercials for Brazil, from ANFAVEA.

How it works

Brazil's figures come from ANFAVEA, the national manufacturers' association, which compiles them from the Renavam / Senatran registration records. Since ANFAVEA's September 2026 site relaunch our fetcher queries its interactive «Central de Dados» (registrations by fuel, Total Leves = Automóveis + Comerciais Leves, monthly); until then it parsed the yearly Excel workbook (sheet «III. Emplacamento Combustível»), which covered the same scope.

The Central de Dados also reports a small pure-ethanol (Etanol) category that the old workbook did not; it is stored in OTHERS (combustion side). Rows fetched before September 2026 carry OTHERS = 0.

Brazil is the one country where FLEXFUEL is its own column and the largest one: most Brazilian cars run on any petrol/ethanol blend. It counts as combustion in the three-curve view, not as an alternative fuel.

Render

Each new or changed month re-renders the country. Shares are read from the CSV as column ÷ TOTAL and grouped into three curves. A generalized Weibull S-curve is fitted to the BEV and the ICE share, and the charts, the fitted parameters, the twelve-month totals and the uncertainty bands are rewritten.

  • BEV→BEV
  • PHEV or EREV (plug-in hybrids, range extenders)

Caveats

  • Cars and light commercials are combined — this is not a passenger-car-only series like most of the gallery.
  • ANFAVEA revises the running year; each run re-reads it and overwrites those months, so recent values can shift slightly between runs.
  • ANFAVEA publishes registrations by brand only as a total, not by fuel, and no models — so there is no «who sells the electrified cars» breakdown for Brazil.

How it works

Brazil's figures come from ANFAVEA, the national manufacturers' association, published as one Excel workbook per year that our fetcher downloads and parses — sheet «III. Emplacamento Combustível», the Unidades table for Automóveis e Comerciais Leves.

Brazil is the one country where FLEXFUEL is its own column and the largest one: most Brazilian cars run on any petrol/ethanol blend. It counts as combustion in the three-curve view, not as an alternative fuel.

Caveats

  • Cars and light commercials are combined — this is not a passenger-car-only series like most of the gallery.
  • Association aggregate: a non-member brand (historically BYD across Latin America) can be under-counted in the BEV figure.
  • Delivered as a yearly Excel workbook; mid-year revised editions (the `-N` suffixed files) are intentionally ignored.

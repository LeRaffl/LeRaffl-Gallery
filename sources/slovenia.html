About the ACEA figures

This country's monthly total comes from ACEA's Europe-wide new-car-registrations press release — one PDF covering the EU, EFTA and the UK, parsed into per-country rows. It is an industry-association aggregate, not a direct national-registry feed: it counts registrations reported through ACEA, is published with a lag (usually the third to fourth week of the following month), and carries only the fuel split ACEA itself reports. Where a national registry is the richer source of record (e.g. Norway's OFV, Switzerland's BFS), we still ingest the ACEA figure here for consistency across the cluster.

Freshness. Latest period in our store: 2026-06. Update cadence: daily cron, 16th–end of month, 08:40 UTC — ACEA publishes in the third to fourth week.

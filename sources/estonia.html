The gallery fits a generalized Weibull curve to the BEV share of each period: share(t) = 1 − exp(v1 · (t − t0)v2), t in calendar years. Where the numbers come from

First registrations in Estonia straight from Transpordiamet, the Transport Administration that keeps the traffic register — one workbook a month with every new vehicle, its EU category and its EU hybrid type, so BEV, PHEV and HEV are counted, not estimated. New cars (Whole), private and company buyers, used imports, vans, lorries and buses, about three weeks before ACEA publishes the month.

Pick the vehicles

The "Uued sõidukid" sheet lists every new vehicle of the month, grouped into rows of identical vehicles with a count. Its EU category decides the variant; the owner type splits new cars into Private and Industry. Used imports are not in this sheet — they are all first registrations of passenger cars minus the new ones, from the "Kytus_esm" sheet.

Powertrain

New vehicles carry the engine type (Mootori tüüp) and, from 2021, the EU hybrid type (Hübriidi tüüp, certificate of conformity field 23.1). The hybrid type decides first; the engine type decides the rest. Used imports have the engine type only.

EU hybrid type, then engine type?
  • OVC-FCHV / NOVC-FCHV or engine VESINIK (fuel cell, hydrogen)→OTHERS
  • OVC-HEV (externally chargeable)→PHEV
    new vehicles only; a used import's hybrid is one combined HEV
  • NOVC-HEV (full or mild hybrid)→HEV
  • engine ELEKTER→BEV
  • engine BENSIIN_HYBRIID / DIISEL_HYBRIID without a hybrid type→HEV
    a few new cars a year; every used hybrid
  • engine BENSIIN, BENSIIN_KATALYSAATOR (incl. LPG / CNG bi-fuel kits)→PETROL
  • engine DIISEL→DIESEL
  • anything else — dedicated CNG or gas engine→OTHERS

Checks before writing

The workbook checks itself. New passenger cars counted from "Uued sõidukid" must equal the headline table's "sh uued" (of which new) passenger cars, and so must the "Kytus_esm" sheet's; all first registrations of passenger cars must equal its "Kokku"; new vans + lorries and new buses must equal their headline columns. A difference above 0.5% (and 3 vehicles) stops the run. Unknown engine, hybrid or owner values may not exceed 2% of a month, and a month below a quarter of the usual volume is not written. 69 of 69 months 2021-01 … 2026-09 pass; 68 match exactly, one (2024-07) by one car.

Definitions & scope

AcquisitionFile — downloaded as a data file (XLSX / Parquet / ODS / zip)
Vehicle scopeVehicles first registered in Estonia in the month — new ones from the register's list of new vehicles, used imports as the difference between all first registrations and the new ones. Vehicles deregistered since are still counted in the month they were registered.
HEV (full hybrids)PHEV and HEV come from the register's EU hybrid type of each new vehicle (certificate of conformity field 23.1) — OVC-HEV (externally chargeable) is PHEV, NOVC-HEV (not externally chargeable — full and mild hybrids) is HEV, as at ACEA. Fuel-cell vehicles (OVC/NOVC-FCHV) are OTHERS. A handful of hybrids a year have no hybrid type recorded; they are counted as HEV. Used imports carry engine types only, so their hybrids stay one combined number in HEV (no PHEV column; the chart draws no PHEV curve) — the Lithuania / Türkiye convention.
BackfillWhole is ACEA to 2020-12 (quarterly) and Transpordiamet from 2021-01 — the first workbook with the EU hybrid type. Private, Industry, Used, Vans, HDV and Buses from 2021-01; the quarterly ACEA rows of Vans/HDV/Buses are kept in data/Estonia_legacy.csv.

Caveats

  • Whole runs about 0.6% above ACEA's Estonian figure (2022–2026), almost all of it in HEV and petrol; BEV and PHEV agree to within a few cars a month. December 2024 — the rush before the 2025 car tax — is 151 cars (4%) above ACEA. In January 2025 ACEA's BEV figure includes 25 fuel-cell cars that this source counts as OTHERS.
  • Bi-fuel cars with a petrol engine and an LPG or CNG kit count as PETROL — the register's engine type decides, which is also how ACEA's figure (the legacy series) counts them. Cars with a dedicated CNG engine are OTHERS.
  • Used: hybrids are one combined number (HEV column, PHEV absent) — the first-registration sheet has engine types only, no hybrid type. Used imports are a market of their own: 11–12% of them were BEV in 2025–2026, more than among new cars.
  • Buses: a few to about 70 vehicles a month, so single fleet deliveries (electric city buses in 2024-05 and 2026-06/07) swing the shares.
  • The step at 2021-01 is a change of source, not of definition: ACEA's Estonian figures are the same register (2021 quarters: within 3%, BEV and PHEV within a few cars), but they were quarterly before 2022.
  • Printed under the charts: New passenger cars (M1); Transpordiamet register from 2021, ACEA (quarterly) before. HEV includes mild hybrids.
